Track A • Comprehensive Theory

📚 Tóm tắt Lý thuyết chuẩn Syllabus 0450 (Units 1 – 6)

Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

Key Concepts: Needs vs. Wants, Scarcity, Opportunity Cost, Factors of Production (Land, Labour, Capital, Enterprise), Added Value, Specialisation, Primary/Secondary/Tertiary sectors, and Business Growth (Internal vs. External, Sole trader to PLC).

Unit 2: People in Business

Key Concepts: Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor), Financial rewards (Wage, Salary, Bonus, Commission, Profit-sharing), Non-financial motivators (Job rotation, enlargement, enrichment), Organisational structures (Span of control, Chain of command, Delegation, Tall vs. Flat), Recruitment & Training, and Legal controls over employment.

Unit 3: Marketing

Key Concepts: Market segmentation, Mass vs. Niche markets, Primary & Secondary market research, The Marketing Mix (4Ps: Product, Price, Place, Promotion), Product Life Cycle (PLC) & Extension strategies, Pricing strategies (Skimming, Penetration, Cost-plus, Competitive, Promotional), Channels of distribution, and E-commerce.

Unit 4: Operations Management

Key Concepts: Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow production), Lean production (Kaizen, Just-in-Time, Kanban, Waste reduction), Economies & Diseconomies of scale, Location decisions, and Quality control vs. Quality assurance.

Unit 5: Financial Information & Financial Decisions

Key Concepts: Sources of finance (Internal vs. External, Short-term vs. Long-term), Cash flow forecasting (Inflows, Outflows, Net cash flow, Closing balance), Break-even analysis (Contribution, Margin of safety), and Income Statements & Balance Sheets (Profitability and Liquidity ratios).

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

Key Concepts: Government economic policies (Inflation, Unemployment, Economic growth, Exchange rates, Taxation, Interest rates), Environmental pressures & Sustainability, and Ethical issues in business decision-making.

Track B • Interactive Exam Practice

Question Set: GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF) - Paper 1

🏢 Business Context:

Tariq is an entrepreneur who founded GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF) as a sole trader, producing handcrafted wooden tables. He faces scarcity of skilled labor and high raw material costs. He is considering converting GLF into a Private Limited Company (Ltd) and expanding operations.

(a) Define 'opportunity cost'. [2 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án chuẩn: The next best alternative foregone by making a choice [2 marks].

(b) Identify two ways in which GLF could add value to its products. [2 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án chuẩn: Branded packaging, high-quality finishing, custom wood carving [2 marks].

(c) Outline two benefits to Tariq of converting GLF from a sole trader to a Private Limited Company (Ltd). [4 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án chuẩn: Limited liability protects personal assets; easier to raise capital by selling shares [4 marks].
