Syllabus 0450 Core Content

📚 Comprehensive Revision Notes (Units 1 to 6)

Review academic summaries across all six functional areas:

Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

Needs, wants, scarcity, opportunity cost, added value, economic sectors, enterprise, business growth, and business organisation types.

Unit 2: People in Business

Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor), financial/non-financial rewards, organisational structures, span of control, delegation, and leadership.

Unit 3: Marketing

Market segmentation, primary & secondary research, Marketing Mix (4Ps), Product Life Cycle, pricing strategies, and e-commerce.

Unit 4: Operations Management

Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow), lean production (Kaizen, JIT), economies of scale, break-even analysis, and quality control.

Unit 5: Financial Information & Financial Decisions

Sources of finance, cash flow forecasting, working capital, income statements, statements of financial position, and accounting ratios.

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

Economic cycles, government economic policies (taxation, interest rates, exchange rates), environmental concerns, globalisation, and MNCs.

Master formal Assessment Objectives (AO1 to AO4):

Demonstrate precise academic understanding of terms and theories.

AO2: Application (20%)
