Track A • Comprehensive Theory

📚 Tóm tắt Lý thuyết chuẩn Syllabus 0450 (Units 1 – 6)

Bấm vào từng Unit để xem tóm tắt kiến thức trọng tâm:

Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

Needs vs. Wants, Scarcity, Opportunity Cost, Factors of Production, Added Value, Specialisation, Business Growth.

Unit 2: People in Business

Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor), Financial & Non-financial rewards, Organisational structures, Recruitment.

Unit 3: Marketing

Market segmentation, Mass vs Niche, Market research, Marketing Mix (4Ps), Product Life Cycle, Pricing strategies, E-commerce.

Unit 4: Operations Management

Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow), Lean production, Economies/Diseconomies of scale, Location decisions.

Unit 5: Financial Information & Decisions

Sources of finance, Cash flow forecasting, Break-even analysis, Income statements & balance sheets.

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

Government economic policies (Inflation, Exchange rates, Taxation), Environmental pressures, Ethics.

Track B • Paper 1 Practice

Question Set: GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF)

🏢 Business Context:

Tariq is an entrepreneur who founded GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF) as a sole trader, producing handcrafted wooden tables. He faces scarcity of skilled labor and high raw material costs. He is considering converting GLF into a Private Limited Company (Ltd) and expanding operations.

(a) Define 'opportunity cost'. [2 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án chuẩn: The next best alternative foregone by making a choice [2 marks].

(b) Identify two ways in which GLF could add value to its products. [2 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án chuẩn: Branded packaging, high-quality finishing, custom wood carving [2 marks].

(c) Outline two benefits to Tariq of converting GLF from a sole trader to a Private Limited Company (Ltd). [4 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án chuẩn: Limited liability protects personal assets; easier to raise capital by selling shares [4 marks].

Track B • Paper 2 Case Study

Question Set: MegaRetail PLC (12-Mark Evaluation)

🏢 Case Context:

MegaRetail PLC operates large supermarkets with falling profits. CEO Thomas considers two options: Option A: Open 50 small convenience stores. Option B: Invest heavily in an e-commerce website and home delivery.

Evaluation: Recommend whether MegaRetail PLC should choose Option A or Option B. Justify your answer. [12 marks]
Xem Gợi ý Mark Scheme

Đáp án chuẩn: Cần phân tích ưu/nhược điểm cả 2 option và đưa ra kết luận lựa chọn có căn cứ (AO4) dựa trên xu hướng thương mại điện tử.
