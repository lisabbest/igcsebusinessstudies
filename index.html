Syllabus 0450 Core Content

📚 Comprehensive Revision Notes (Units 1 to 6)

Review core concepts across all six functional areas:

Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

Needs, wants, scarcity, opportunity cost, added value, economic sectors, enterprise, business growth, and types of business organisation (Sole traders, Partnerships, Ltd, PLC).

Unit 2: People in Business

Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor), financial/non-financial rewards, organisational structures, span of control, delegation, leadership styles, recruitment, and training.

Unit 3: Marketing

Market segmentation, primary & secondary research, Marketing Mix (4Ps), Product Life Cycle, pricing strategies, distribution channels, promotion, and e-commerce.

Unit 4: Operations Management

Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow), lean production (Kaizen, JIT), economies of scale, break-even analysis, quality control/assurance, and location decisions.

Unit 5: Financial Information & Financial Decisions

Sources of finance, cash flow forecasting, working capital, income statements, statement of financial position (balance sheets), and liquidity/profitability ratios.

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

Business cycles, government economic policies (taxation, interest rates, exchange rates), environmental concerns, ethical issues, globalisation, and multinational companies (MNCs).

🏢 Case Study Insert: Apex Logistics PLC (ALL)

Apex Logistics PLC (ALL) is a large courier company operating in country Z. Managing Director Sarah wants to expand into a new region.
Appendix 1: Forecasted financial data for Internal Growth (building a depot) vs External Growth (takeover of SwiftDeliver Ltd).
Appendix 2: Employee survey results showing low motivation among delivery drivers.

Question 1 (20 Marks Total)

(a) Identify two features of internal growth.[2 marks]
(b) Explain two reasons why delivery driver motivation is low at ALL (refer to Appendix 2).[8 marks]
(c) Explain two advantages and two disadvantages of a tall organisational structure for ALL.[10 marks]

Question 2 (20 Marks Total)

(a) Identify two sources of long-term finance suitable for a PLC.[2 marks]
(b) Explain two factors Sarah should consider when choosing between a bank loan and issuing new shares.[6 marks]
(c) Analyse the importance of cash flow forecasting to ALL when expanding operations.[12 marks]

Question 3 (20 Marks Total)

(a) Identify two methods of primary market research.[2 marks]
(b) Explain two advantages and two disadvantages to ALL of using e-commerce for booking deliveries.[8 marks]
(c) Analyse the impact of an increase in interest rates on ALL's financial performance.[10 marks]

Question 4 (20 Marks Total - 12-Mark Evaluation)

(a) Identify two potential problems of business growth.[2 marks]
(b) Explain two reasons why a takeover of SwiftDeliver Ltd might fail.[6 marks]
(c) Recommend whether Apex Logistics PLC should choose internal growth or external growth (takeover). Justify your answer using Appendix 1 and other information.[12 marks]

⭐ Achieving Excellence in Cambridge IGCSE Business Studies

Master the formal Assessment Objectives (AO1 to AO4) mandated by Cambridge examiners:

AO1: Knowledge & Understanding (40%)

Demonstrate precise knowledge and academic understanding of business facts, terms, concepts, and theories.

AO2: Application (20%)

Apply knowledge and understanding effectively by integrating given case study data and business contexts into your arguments.

AO3: Analysis (25%)

Order, interpret, and analyse financial, numerical, and narrative information using rigorous cause-and-effect chains.

AO4: Evaluation (15%)

Present reasoned explanations, develop balanced arguments, and draw justified, evidence-based business judgements and recommendations.
