Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

Economic cycles, government economic policies (taxation, interest rates, exchange rates), environmental concerns, globalisation, and MNCs.

A* Examination Technique Masterclass

⭐ Guidance & Instructions to Write A* Answers by Question Type

Follow these precise Cambridge academic frameworks to secure top marks across all components:

1. Define / State / Identify [2 Marks]

Assessment Objective: AO1 (Knowledge - 40%)

A* Writing Instruction: Give the precise academic definition or name key business terms directly. Do not waste time writing case study examples. Keep answers concise (1-2 sentences max).

2. Outline / Describe [4 Marks]

Assessment Objective: AO1 (2 marks) + AO2 (Application - 20%)

A* Writing Instruction: Use a strict 2-part structure. State one clear business point/benefit (1 mark) and immediately apply it to the case business context (1 mark).

3. Explain [6 or 8 Marks]

Assessment Objective: AO1 (2) + AO2 (2) + AO3 (Analysis - 25%)

A* Writing Instruction: Master the K → APP → AN formula: State knowledge, weave in case context, and trace economic consequences to profits/costs.

4. Justify / Recommend / Evaluate [12 Marks (Paper 2)]

Assessment Objective: AO1 + AO2 + AO3 + AO4 (Evaluation - 15%)

A* Writing Instruction: Compare options in context using appendices, weigh short-term vs long-term risks, and draw a justified final recommendation.
