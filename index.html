📚 Comprehensive Revision Notes (Units 1 to 6)

Review academic summaries across all six functional areas required for Cambridge examinations[cite: 10]:

1. Understanding Business Activity

Needs, wants, scarcity, opportunity cost, added value, economic sectors, enterprise, business growth, objectives, and stakeholder interests[cite: 10].

2. People in Business

Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor), financial/non-financial rewards, organisational structures, span of control, delegation, leadership, recruitment, and training[cite: 10].

3. Marketing

Market segmentation, primary & secondary research, Marketing Mix (4Ps), Product Life Cycle, pricing strategies, and e-commerce[cite: 10].

4. Operations Management

Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow), lean production (Kaizen, JIT), economies of scale, break-even analysis, and quality assurance/control[cite: 10].

5. Financial Information and Decisions

Sources of finance, cash flow forecasting, working capital, income statements, statements of financial position, and liquidity/profitability ratios[cite: 10].

6. External Influences on Business Activity

Economic cycles, government economic policies (taxation, interest rates, exchange rates), environmental concerns, globalisation, and MNCs[cite: 10].
