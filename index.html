Course Aims & Objectives

Designed to develop critical thinking, numerical literacy, and decision-making skills across diverse business environments[cite: 6].

Assessment Components: All candidates complete Paper 1 (Short Answer & Data Response) and Paper 2 (Case Study). Each component is 1 hour 30 minutes, worth 80 marks (50% weighting each)[cite: 10].

Track A • Comprehensive Syllabus Notes

📚 Complete IGCSE Business Studies Core Theory (Units 1–6)

Click each unit to review academic summaries across all functional areas[cite: 10]:

Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

Needs, wants, scarcity, opportunity cost, added value, economic sectors, enterprise, business growth, and business organisation structures (Sole traders, Partnerships, Ltd, PLC)[cite: 10].

Unit 2: People in Business

Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor), financial/non-financial rewards, organisational structures, span of control, delegation, leadership styles, recruitment, and training[cite: 10].

Unit 3: Marketing

Market segmentation, primary & secondary research, Marketing Mix (4Ps), Product Life Cycle, pricing strategies, distribution channels, and e-commerce[cite: 10].

Unit 4: Operations Management

Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow), lean production (Kaizen, JIT), economies of scale, break-even analysis, quality control/assurance, and location decisions[cite: 10].

Unit 5: Financial Information & Financial Decisions

Sources of finance, cash flow forecasting, working capital, income statements, statement of financial position, and liquidity/profitability ratios[cite: 10].

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

Economic cycles, government economic policies (taxation, interest rates, exchange rates), environmental concerns, ethical issues, globalisation, and MNCs[cite: 10].
