📚 Toàn bộ Lý thuyết Trọng tâm (Units 1 – 6)

Tổng hợp các định nghĩa, từ vựng cốt lõi (AO1) và sơ đồ tư duy phục vụ ôn thi:

Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

• Needs vs Wants: Needs are essential for survival; wants are unlimited desires.

• Economic Problem: Scarcity of resources (Land, Labour, Capital, Enterprise) vs unlimited wants $\rightarrow$ Opportunity Cost.

• Added Value: Selling Price - Cost of Raw Materials. (Crucial: Added value is NOT profit).

• Business Growth: Internal (organic) vs External (mergers & takeovers). Sole trader to PLC conversion benefits.

Unit 2: People in Business

• Motivation Theories: Maslow (Hierarchy of Needs), Herzberg (Two-Factor Theory), Taylor (Scientific Management & piece-rate).

• Financial Rewards: Wages, salaries, bonus, commission, profit-sharing.

• Non-Financial Rewards: Job rotation, job enlargement, job enrichment, teamwork.

• Organisational Structure: Chain of command, span of control, tall vs flat hierarchies, delegation.

Unit 3: Marketing

• Market Segmentation: Grouping customers by age, income, lifestyle, location.

• Market Research: Primary (surveys, interviews, focus groups) vs Secondary (internet, government reports, internal data).

• Marketing Mix (4Ps): Product, Price (Skimming, Penetration, Cost-plus, Competitive), Place (Channels of distribution), Promotion (Advertising, BOGOF).

• Product Life Cycle: Introduction $\rightarrow$ Growth $\rightarrow$ Maturity $\rightarrow$ Saturation $\rightarrow$ Decline + Extension strategies.

Unit 4: Operations Management

• Production Methods: Job production (one-off), Batch production (groups), Flow production (mass continuous).

• Lean Production: Kaizen (continuous improvement), Just-in-Time (JIT), minimizing waste and holding costs.

• Economies of Scale: Purchasing, financial, managerial economies lowering average costs as scale increases.

Unit 5: Financial Information & Decisions

• Sources of Finance: Internal (retained earnings) vs External (bank loans, share issues, overdrafts, trade credit).

• Cash Flow Forecasting: Inflows, outflows, net cash flow, closing balance. Managing working capital deficits.

• Break-Even Analysis: Fixed costs, variable costs, contribution, margin of safety.

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

• Government Economic Policies: Economic growth, inflation, unemployment, exchange rates, interest rates, and taxation.

• Environmental & Ethical Issues: Sustainability, carbon footprint, pressure groups, and ethical decision-making.

🏢 Business Context:

Tariq founded GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF) as a sole trader, producing handcrafted wooden tables. He faces scarcity of skilled labor and high raw material costs. He is considering converting GLF into a Private Limited Company (Ltd).

(a) Define 'opportunity cost'. [2 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án: The next best alternative foregone by making a choice [2 marks].

(c) Outline two benefits to Tariq of converting GLF from a sole trader to a Private Limited Company (Ltd). [4 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án: Limited liability protects personal assets; easier to raise capital by selling shares [4 marks].

⭐ Hướng Dẫn Viết Bài Đạt Điểm A* Chuẩn Mark Scheme

Bí quyết chinh phục trọn vẹn các Mục tiêu Đánh giá (Assessment Objectives):

AO1: Knowledge (30%)

Thuộc lòng định nghĩa và thuật ngữ kinh doanh. Trả lời chính xác và ngắn gọn ở các câu hỏi Define/State (2 marks).

AO2: Application (30%)

Bắt buộc phải lồng ghép tên doanh nghiệp (GLF, BCF, MegaRetail) hoặc chi tiết đề bài vào trong mỗi luận điểm.

AO3: Analysis (20%)

Dùng cấu trúc "Because $\rightarrow$ Leading to $\rightarrow$ Resulting in profits/costs" để giải thích chuỗi hệ quả logic.

AO4: Evaluation (20%)

Ở các câu 12 marks Paper 2, phải đưa ra lựa chọn dứt khoát và giải thích vì sao phương án đó ưu việt hơn trong dài hạn.

💡 Công thức "K - APP - AN" chuẩn Examiner cho câu 6 marks:

1. Knowledge (K): Nêu ý nghĩa hoặc lợi ích.

2. Application (APP): Gắn trực tiếp vào sản phẩm gỗ, thợ mộc của doanh nghiệp.

3. Analysis (AN): Hệ quả tài chính (lợi nhuận tăng hay chi phí giảm).
