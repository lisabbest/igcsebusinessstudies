Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

• Needs, wants, scarcity, and opportunity cost.

Unit 2: People in Business

• Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor).

• Financial vs Non-financial rewards, organisation structures, span of control, delegation, and recruitment.

Unit 3: Marketing

• Market segmentation, primary & secondary research, Marketing Mix (4Ps), Product Life Cycle, and E-commerce.

Unit 4: Operations Management

• Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow), Lean production (Kaizen, JIT), economies of scale, and quality control.

Unit 5: Financial Information & Financial Decisions

• Sources of finance, cash flow forecasting, break-even analysis, income statements, and liquidity/profitability ratios.

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

• Government economic policies (inflation, exchange rates, taxation), environmental issues, and globalisation / MNCs.

Official Paper 1 • Short Answer & Data Response

Mock Paper 1: GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF)

Total Marks: 80

🏢 Stimulus Material:

GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF) is a private limited company based in country X. Tariq is the managing director and founder. GLF uses batch production to manufacture wooden dining tables and chairs, employing 40 skilled workers. The local economy is currently experiencing an economic boom, leading to rising incomes. However, inflation is also increasing, raising timber raw material costs.

(a) Define 'private limited company'. [2 marks]
Mark Scheme (AO1)

A business owned by shareholders with limited liability, shares cannot be sold to the general public [2 marks]. (1 mark for partial definition).

(b) Identify two features of batch production used by GLF. [2 marks]
Mark Scheme (AO1/AO2)

1 mark per valid feature (e.g., goods produced in sets/batches, flexible machinery, some standardisation) [2 marks].

(c) Outline two possible problems for GLF of high inflation. [4 marks]
Mark Scheme (AO1/AO2)

1 mark for identifying problem + 1 mark for application to GLF timber costs/tables (Max 2 marks x 2 = 4 marks).

(d) Explain one advantage and one disadvantage to GLF of having a well-motivated workforce of 40 skilled workers. [6 marks]
Mark Scheme (AO1/AO2/AO3)

K (2) + APP (2) + AN (2). Advantage: Higher labor productivity in making wooden tables. Disadvantage: Higher financial reward costs (bonuses/wages).

Official Paper 2 • Case Study & Evaluation

Mock Paper 2: Apex Logistics PLC (ALL)

Total Marks: 80

📊 Case Study Background & Appendices:

Apex Logistics PLC (ALL) is a delivery transport firm experiencing rising demand. Managing Director Sarah wants to expand.
Appendix 1: Financial data comparing internal growth (building a new depot) vs external growth (taking over a rival firm).
Appendix 2: Employee turnover statistics showing dissatisfaction among delivery drivers.

Evaluation Question: Recommend whether Apex Logistics PLC should choose internal growth or external growth (takeover) to expand its operations. Justify your answer. [12 marks]
Mark Scheme Guidance (AO1–AO4)

Must analyze benefits/drawbacks of both options in context of ALL logistics, provide reasoned arguments, and draw a supported final judgment/recommendation.

Examiner Masterclass

⭐ Command Words & AO1–AO4 Breakdown

Understand exactly what Cambridge examiners look for in your answers:

Define [2 marks]

Give the precise meaning. No context or examples required unless specified.

Identify / State [1-2 marks]

Name, select, or express in clear terms without lengthy explanations.

Outline / Explain [4-6 marks]

Set out main points or explain cause-and-effect relationships backed with case application (APP).

Justify / Recommend [6-12 marks]

Support a case with evidence, compare options, and draw a justified final judgment (AO4).
