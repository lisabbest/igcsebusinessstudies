Track A • Unit 1 Theory

Chapter 1.1: Business Activity & The Economic Problem

Needs vs. Wants: Needs are essentials for survival (food, water). Wants are desires for comfort. Resources are limited, but wants are infinite.

Opportunity Cost: The next best alternative foregone when making a choice.

Added Value: Selling price minus cost of raw materials (Added value $\neq$ profit).

Track B • Interactive Exam Practice

GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF) - Paper 1 Practice Set

🏢 Business Context:

Tariq is an entrepreneur who founded GreenLeaf Furniture (GLF) as a sole trader, producing handcrafted wooden tables. He faces scarcity of skilled labor and high raw material costs. He is considering converting GLF into a Private Limited Company (Ltd) and expanding operations.

(a) Define 'opportunity cost'. [2 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án: The next best alternative foregone by making a choice [2 marks].

(b) Identify two ways in which GLF could add value to its products. [2 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án: Branded packaging, high-quality finishing, custom wood carving [2 marks].

(c) Outline two benefits to Tariq of converting GLF from a sole trader to a Private Limited Company (Ltd). [4 marks]
Xem Mark Scheme

Đáp án: Limited liability protects personal assets; easier to raise capital by selling shares [4 marks].
