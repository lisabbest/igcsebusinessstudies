Syllabus 0450 Core Theory

📚 Comprehensive Revision Notes (Units 1 to 6)

Expand each unit to review academic definitions and core concepts:

Unit 1: Understanding Business Activity

Needs, wants, scarcity, opportunity cost, added value, economic sectors, and business organization structures.

Unit 2: People in Business

Motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor), financial/non-financial rewards, organizational structures, span of control, delegation, and recruitment.

Unit 3: Marketing

Market segmentation, primary & secondary research, Marketing Mix (4Ps), Product Life Cycle, pricing strategies, and e-commerce.

Unit 4: Operations Management

Production methods (Job, Batch, Flow), lean production (Kaizen, JIT), economies of scale, quality control, and location decisions.

Unit 5: Financial Information & Financial Decisions

Sources of finance, cash flow forecasting, break-even analysis, income statements, statements of financial position, and accounting ratios.

Unit 6: External Influences on Business Activity

Government economic policies (taxation, interest rates, exchange rates), environmental sustainability, globalisation, and multinational companies.

Component 2 • Case Study Paper (1 hour 30 minutes)

📊 Full Paper 2: Apex Logistics PLC (ALL)

Total: 80 Marks (50%)

This official paper comprises four comprehensive questions based on the case study insert and appendices below. Answer all questions.

🏢 Case Study Background & Appendices: Apex Logistics PLC (ALL)

Apex Logistics PLC (ALL) is a national courier and delivery firm experiencing high shipping demand. Managing Director Sarah wants to expand operations into a neighboring region. Employee turnover among delivery drivers is high.

Appendix 1: Financial data comparing Internal Growth (building a new regional depot from scratch) versus External Growth (taking over a smaller local rival courier firm).

Appendix 2: Results of an employee survey showing driver dissatisfaction with autocratic leadership and repetitive routing duties.

Q1 (a) Identify two features of internal growth. [2 marks]
Q1 (b) Explain two reasons why ALL's delivery drivers are dissatisfied. [8 marks] [10 marks total]
Mark Scheme Guidance (AO1/AO2/AO3)

Q1(a): Expansion using own resources, no takeovers [2 marks]. Q1(b): Autocratic leadership, repetitive tasks, lack of delegation linked to Appendix 2 [8 marks].

Q2 (a) Identify two sources of finance suitable for a PLC. [2 marks]
Q2 (b) Explain two factors Sarah should consider when choosing between a bank loan and issuing shares. [6 marks] [8 marks total]
Mark Scheme Guidance (AO1/AO2/AO3)

Q2(a): Issue of shares, debentures [2 marks]. Q2(b): Amount required, interest repayments, and dilution of control for ALL [6 marks].

Q3 (a) Identify two methods of primary market research ALL could use. [2 marks]
Q3 (b) Explain two advantages and two disadvantages to ALL of using e-commerce for parcel bookings. [8 marks] [10 marks total]
Mark Scheme Guidance (AO1/AO2/AO3)

Q3(a): Online surveys, customer interviews [2 marks]. Q3(b): 2 advantages (global reach, 24/7 booking) + 2 disadvantages (cybersecurity risk, delivery logistics costs) [8 marks].

Q4 (12-Mark Evaluation) Recommend whether Apex Logistics PLC should choose internal growth or external growth (takeover). Justify your answer using Appendix 1 and other information. [12 marks]
Mark Scheme Guidance (AO1–AO4)

Must analyze benefits/drawbacks of both options in context of ALL logistics, provide reasoned arguments, and draw a supported final judgment/recommendation.

⭐ Achieving Excellence in Cambridge IGCSE Business Studies

Master the formal Assessment Objectives (AO1 to AO4) mandated by Cambridge examiners:

AO1: Knowledge & Understanding (40%)

Demonstrate precise knowledge and academic understanding of business facts, terms, concepts, and theories.

AO2: Application (20%)

Apply knowledge and understanding effectively by integrating given case study data and business contexts into your arguments.

AO3: Analysis (25%)

Order, interpret, and analyse financial, numerical, and narrative information using rigorous cause-and-effect chains.

AO4: Evaluation (15%)

Present reasoned explanations, develop balanced arguments, and draw justified, evidence-based business judgements and recommendations.
